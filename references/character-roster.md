# Character Roster

Use the canonical character name for personal fan-expression or licensed output. For public commercial work without permission, use the original archetype label and keep the canonical mapping only in the private analysis.

## Core roster

### spongebob — SpongeBob / 海绵宝宝

- Conversation signals: energetic follow-through, eager iteration, service instinct, visible optimism, keeps helping after the task grows.
- Workplace archetype: **全自动补锅机** / tireless fixer.
- Strength: turns vague needs into finished work and keeps morale alive.
- Workplace wound: reliability becomes an unlimited subscription; extra work arrives disguised as trust.
- Voice: bright, earnest, funny through contrast rather than cynicism.
- Title bank: 深海全自动补锅机；快乐但工伤；需求海绵吸收器；工位太阳能板.
- Palette: `#FFD84D` accent, `#79D9D4` secondary, `#FF7B6B` pop.

### squidward — Squidward / 章鱼哥

- Workplace archetype: **工位清醒者** / reluctant professional.

- Workplace wound: competent enough to be pulled in, self-aware enough to suffer twice.
- Voice: dry, restrained, one sharp line beats five jokes.
- Title bank: 工位清醒者；审美救火队；被迫营业艺术家；低电量专业户.
- Palette: `#77C9C5` accent, `#C7A6E8` secondary, `#F2C86B` pop.

## Pairing patterns

Use a secondary match only when it adds explanatory value:

- SpongeBob × Squidward: cheerful execution outside, exhausted standards inside.
- Sandy × Karen: experimental builder with a systems brain.
- Mr. Krabs × Plankton: commercial instinct plus founder-scale ambition.
- Patrick × Gary: low-volume communication with unexpected clarity.
- Mrs. Puff × Bubble Bass: risk control plus exacting acceptance criteria.
- Pearl × Larry: expressive culture energy plus visible momentum.

Avoid turning a pair into a relationship diagnosis. Use it only as a work-style blend.
