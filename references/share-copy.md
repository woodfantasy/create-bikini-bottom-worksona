# Xiaohongshu Share Copy

## Content principle

Lead with identity and emotion, not the rendering technology. The most shareable structure is:

1. “I got read accurately” hook.
2. One workplace contradiction.
3. Two or three proof details.
4. A line other users can borrow.
5. A question inviting character comparison.

## Title patterns

Keep titles concise and natural. Adapt, do not copy mechanically:

- Agent 根据当前可见对话，说我是「深海全自动补锅机」
- 原来我的打工人格不是卷，是海绵宝宝型兜底
- 努力工作的人，为什么最后总在补别人的锅
- 被 Agent 做成章鱼哥人设卡后，我沉默了
- 测完才知道：我的剩余电量和责任心完全不成比例

Avoid absolute claims such as “100% accurate,” “scientific,” or “AI knows you better than anyone.”

## Tone levels

- Gentle: warm recognition, no direct blame.
- Dry: restrained sarcasm and one sharp boundary.
- Spicy: stronger workplace contrast, still no named attacks or harassment.

Default to dry for Squidward, Karen, Bubble Bass, and Mrs. Puff; warm for SpongeBob, Patrick, Gary, and Pearl; energetic for Sandy, Larry, Mr. Krabs, and Plankton.

## Carousel

- Slide 1: full identity card.
- Slide 2: expanded “聊天记录把我卖了,” each receipt paired with a paraphrased conversation behavior.
- Slide 3: secondary match, rematch prompt, and a “save/share” question.

Do not put more than one dominant emotional sentence on slide 1.
